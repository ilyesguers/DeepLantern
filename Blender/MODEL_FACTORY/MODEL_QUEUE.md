## Priority meaning
- P0: recognizable, frequently seen gameplay assets.
- P1: major zone identity and environmental variety.
- P2: modular set dressing and station completeness.
- P3: optional life and ambience after core assets are validated.

## Gate before expanding the queue
Do not generate hundreds of near-duplicates. Establish a validated style and performance baseline with DL-001 through DL-006 first. Then continue. Variation should come from authored silhouettes, material palettes, scale, damage states and placement—not merely random hue changes.
