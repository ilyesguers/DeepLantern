# DL-017 — Navigation Signage Kit
**Priority:** P2 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Set
Blank modular signboards: wall plaque, hanging directional sign, floor/route marker, hazard stripe panel, and small numbered-equipment plate. Text and arrows should remain replaceable with Roblox UI/decal; don't bake words into textures.

## Style
High contrast: warm off-white plate, deep navy frame, safety orange accents, cyan navigation marks. Thick frame and readable shape from distance; avoid tiny unreadable text and brand logos.

## Performance
Low poly, 200–1.5k triangles per piece. Origins should support wall or floor placement; document orientation. Include a preview with temporary placeholder glyphs, but provide blank production plates.

## Prompt
“Modular stylized underwater research station signage kit with blank replaceable plates: wall plaque, hanging directional sign, floor route marker, hazard stripe panel and equipment ID plate. Thick navy frame, warm off-white face, orange safety accents and simple cyan navigation icon areas. Clear silhouette and high contrast at distance, no baked text or logos, low-poly optimized geometry, clean pivots for wall/floor mounting.”
