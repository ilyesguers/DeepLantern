# DL-019 — Small Underwater Fauna Family
**Priority:** P3 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Set
Three non-threatening stylized creatures: tiny reef fish, slow drifting jellyfish, and small shrimp-like cleaner creature. Focus on readable silhouettes and friendly scientific wonder. These are mesh assets only; animation/AI scripts are out of scope unless separately requested.

## Style and rig-readiness
Distinct silhouettes and limited color palettes. Fish has separate body/fins if future rigging is needed; jellyfish bell and tentacles are robust, thick enough to read; shrimp has clear head/body/tail segmentation. Avoid realistic horror features, sharp teeth or swarms of tiny parts.

## Performance
1k–4k triangles per creature. Provide a clean static export and, if feasible, a version with sensible part separation for later rigging. Document whether it is rigged (default: no). Avoid expensive transparent layered materials.

“Three charming premium stylized small underwater creatures for an exploration game: colorful tiny reef fish, graceful jellyfish with a luminous bell and thick readable tentacles, and a small shrimp-like cleaner creature. Friendly and scientifically curious, not cartoonishly flat and not horror.
