# DL-003 — Deployable Dive Beacon / Sonar Buoy

**Priority:** P0  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal and silhouette
A highly readable, deployable navigation and sonar device used by divers to mark discoveries and routes. Compact vertical buoy with a weighted lower body, protective outer cage, luminous top ring, sensor head, side service ports, tether attachment and deployable feet/fins. It must look stable and functional underwater.

## Materials
Matte dark teal/navy shell, warm safety-orange grab/locking elements, brushed titanium rings, translucent cyan light lens and tiny engraved status panels. The glowing ring should be a thin focal accent, not a large overexposed blob.

## Modeling detail
Primary: tapered pressure housing and weighted base. Secondary: cage ribs, top sensor puck, ring housing, service hatch and tether eye. Tertiary: fasteners, recessed seams, embossed symbols without text, cable strain relief. Keep all parts connected or mechanically attached; no floating geometry.

## Game constraints
Readable at medium distance; no tiny fragile antennae. Target 2k–6k triangles, 2–3 materials, 1K texture maximum unless justified. Pivot at bottom center for placement on the seafloor; document total height and footprint.

## Deliverables and checks
.blend, .fbx/.glb, optional maps, 3-view and hero renders, manifest. Check base sits flat, light lens is a distinct material, pivot is at the base, export/re-import preserves normals and materials.

## Generator prompt
“Create a professional stylized underwater deployable sonar navigation beacon for a premium exploration game. Strong recognizable silhouette, believable engineering, layered forms, clean optimized topology, UV-ready PBR materials, no text or logo, no fragile thin antennas, real-time game asset. Pivot at bottom center, flat stable base.”
