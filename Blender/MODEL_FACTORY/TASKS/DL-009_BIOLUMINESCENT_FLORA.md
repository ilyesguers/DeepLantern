# DL-009 — Bioluminescent Flora Family
**Priority:** P1 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Set
Three assets: cup-shaped luminous anemone, clustered bulb flora, and thin-stemmed glowing sea flower. Each should use a non-emissive body plus a restrained emissive core/rim.

## Art direction
Mysterious and beautiful, not horror. Teal/cyan and violet with a small warm amber variant. Glow must not wash out the silhouette. Give each asset a distinct growth pattern and a weighted base that fits on uneven rock.

## Technical targets
1k–4k triangles each; use emissive material only on selected geometry. Avoid excessive transparent layers, tiny tentacles and heavy particle effects. Pivot at base; export geometry/material only, not gameplay scripts.

## Prompt
“Stylized bioluminescent deep-sea flora set for a premium exploration game: a cup-shaped glowing anemone, clustered luminous bulbs and a delicate sea flower. Sculpted organic stems and petals, rich teal/cyan/violet accents with small controlled emissive cores, beautiful readable silhouette, wonder and mystery rather than horror. Game-ready low-to-mid poly mesh, optimized glow surfaces, clean UVs, no excessive transparency or needle-thin tentacles, separate assets with stable ground pivots.”
