# DL-011 — Twilight Reef Landmark Coral Arch
**Priority:** P1 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Goal
A signature landmark for the `Zone_TwilightReef` zone. It must be recognizable from far away and frame a route or vista without blocking navigation unexpectedly.

## Composition
Large naturally curved arch made of fused rock/coral masses, asymmetrical supports, layered coral shelves, luminous growths concentrated at a few focal points, and a clear opening through the center. Make the silhouette interesting from front and side; do not create a perfect symmetrical doorway.

## Optimization
Build a mid-poly hero mesh with high-frequency surface detail baked into maps where possible. Aim 8k–18k triangles depending on silhouette complexity; document final count. Avoid invisible internal geometry and dense procedural branching. Use modular secondary coral pieces only if they are easy to reuse.

## Prompt
“Create an iconic stylized Twilight Reef landmark: a massive asymmetrical natural coral-rock arch with a large clear passage through its center, sculpted layered rock, rich violet and turquoise coral growths, a few controlled bioluminescent cyan veins, subtle suspended sediment pockets. Majestic and mysterious, never horror. Must read as a strong distant silhouette and look compelling from front, side and three-quarter views. Premium game-ready mid-poly geometry, optimized topology, UV-ready PBR, no tiny floating ornaments, no obstructing debris inside the passage.”
