# DL-014 — Submarine Docking Pad and Service Arm
**Priority:** P2 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Goal
A modular landing/docking prop that visually explains how the research submarine is secured and serviced at the station. Do not change the existing docking system or script behavior.

## Parts
Low-profile platform with anti-slip inset panels, four visible guide marks, two chunky cradle supports, one articulated-looking service arm (static mesh unless animation is explicitly requested), cable coupler and maintenance light.

## Constraints
Keep floor-contact plane flat and clear; avoid thin rails that catch on the vehicle. Provide separate pad and arm assets with documented bounds/pivots. Target 3k–8k triangles combined and use shared station materials.
