# DL-012 — Ancient Seafloor Relic / Mystery Structure
**Priority:** P1 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Goal
An environmental discovery landmark that hints at an unknown history without defining new lore or gameplay mechanics on its own.

## Visual language
A partly buried non-human-looking stone/metal structure: broad concentric rings, broken geometric plates, a central sealed aperture and faint cyan mineral seams. It should feel ancient and enigmatic, but not like a real-world religious monument or a weapon. Keep all parts structurally supported.

## Performance
Hero landmark 6k–15k triangles; use material masks for fine runes/scratches instead of thousands of engraved polygons. Pivot at the base; include optional buried-base mesh for terrain integration and a separate clean version.

## Prompt
“Premium stylized underwater ancient mystery relic for an exploration game: broad weathered concentric rings, fractured geometric plates, a sealed central aperture and faint cyan mineral seams, partially designed to appear embedded in seafloor. Strange and awe-inspiring, not horror, not a weapon, no legible text or recognizable religious symbols. Strong layered silhouette, believable stone-metal construction, optimized game topology, UV-ready PBR, clean export with documented pivot and bounds.”
