# DL-015 — Underwater Cave Entrance Kit
**Priority:** P2 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Set
Three compatible pieces: broad rock mouth, overhanging ledge, and side-wall buttress. Include a separate optional mineral/coral accent cluster. Do not build an entire level as a single mesh.

## Design
Readable cave opening with a clear navigable path. Sculpted stratified stone, rounded erosion, a few luminous mineral seams and sparse coral accents. The cave must feel invitingly mysterious, not like a horror mouth or a trap.

## Technical
Each piece 3k–8k triangles; use separate modular pieces and documented snap/ground pivots. Ensure the entrance interior does not contain a blocking invisible surface. Provide a neutral gray blockout preview and a finished material preview.

## Prompt
“Stylized underwater cave entrance modular kit: broad sculpted rock mouth with clear passage, overhanging ledge, side-wall buttress and optional small mineral-coral accent. Layered erosion, rounded edges, subtle teal/cyan mineral veins and sparse coral, dramatic but inviting atmosphere, no horror-mouth silhouette, no obstructing geometry, optimized game-ready mesh, UV-ready PBR, modular separate pieces with documented bounds.”
