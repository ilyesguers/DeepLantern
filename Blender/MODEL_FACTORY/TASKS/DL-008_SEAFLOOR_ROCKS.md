# DL-008 — Seafloor Rock Kit
**Priority:** P1 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Set
Six modular rocks: low rounded boulder, fractured slab, tall split rock, porous volcanic stone, layered sediment shelf, and small scatter-rock cluster.

## Design
Stylized sculpted shapes with intentional planes, bevels and erosion; avoid generic perfect low-poly ico-spheres. Use two shared material families: cool blue-gray stone and warm mineral-tinted stone. Add sparse sediment streaks, pitted cavities and layered strata, keeping major shape readable. No attached coral unless it is a separate optional object.

## Technical targets
1k–4k triangles per hero rock, less for small scatter. Use material/normal detail instead of dense geometry. All pieces need ground-contact pivots, consistent scale, and no hidden bottom surfaces if unnecessary. Include a composition preview demonstrating overlap without visible seams.

## Prompt
“Create six premium stylized underwater seafloor rock assets with authored silhouettes: rounded eroded boulder, fractured slab, tall split rock, porous volcanic rock, layered sediment shelf, small scatter cluster. Sculpted planes and erosion, subtle pitting and strata, cool blue-gray and muted mineral palettes, no perfect spheres, no noisy micro-detail, optimized game topology, UV-ready PBR, separate objects, stable ground pivots.”
