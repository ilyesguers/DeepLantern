# DL-007 — Kelp Cluster and Fronds
**Priority:** P1 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Goal
Create a modular kelp cluster for the Kelp Forest zone, with a strong vertical rhythm and a recognizable silhouette even at low graphics settings.

## Construction
A shared root/base, 5–9 broad ribbon-like fronds with tapered tips, subtle central veins, a few thick stipes, and optional small gas-bladder forms. Make a small, medium and tall arrangement from reusable pieces. Fronds should feel curved and organic, not like flat rectangular strips.

## Palette and lighting
Deep green-teal shadows, sea-glass green midtones, lime highlights near light-facing edges, muted golden accents only sparingly. Avoid emissive glow on all leaves. Use roughness variation and gentle translucency only if it survives Roblox import.

## Performance
Target 1.5k–5k triangles per cluster. Use simple double-sided geometry only where needed and check that fronds do not disappear from normal gameplay angles. Pivot at seafloor root; document bounds and wind-animation suitability. Do not add per-frame simulation scripts.

## Prompt
“Premium stylized kelp forest asset for a Roblox underwater exploration game: elegant tall seaweed cluster with thick anchored root, several gracefully curved ribbon fronds, tapered natural tips, subtle raised central veins and layered depth. Deep teal-green shadows with sea-glass and lime highlights. Strong organic silhouette visible from a distance, no paper-thin flat rectangles, no noisy micro-detail, optimized low-to-mid poly topology, UV-ready PBR, modular fronds and stable bottom pivot.”
