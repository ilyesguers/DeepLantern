# DL-010 — Underwater Wreckage and Research Debris
**Priority:** P1 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Set
A small modular kit: bent metal panel, broken support frame, abandoned sensor housing, cracked pressure door, short pipe segment, and a damaged equipment crate. These are environmental props, not a new gameplay system.

## Style
Suggest a past expedition without becoming horror: restrained corrosion, mineral deposits, bent-but-believable structures, faded research blue, dark metal and a few orange remnants. Avoid gore, skulls, jump-scare shapes, or excessive grime.

## Performance and reuse
500–3k triangles per piece; use shared trim textures/materials. Damage must be part of the silhouette, not just a grunge texture. Keep separate parts modular and ground/contact pivots documented. Avoid open razor-sharp sheet edges that disappear at distance.

## Prompt
“Modular stylized underwater research wreckage set: bent metal panel, broken support frame, abandoned sensor housing, cracked pressure door, corroded pipe segment and damaged equipment crate. Believable engineered construction, controlled wear and marine mineral deposits, faded deep blue paint, dark metal and sparse orange safety remnants. Eerie mystery but not horror, strong readable damage silhouettes, clean optimized game meshes, shared UV-ready materials, no text logos.”
