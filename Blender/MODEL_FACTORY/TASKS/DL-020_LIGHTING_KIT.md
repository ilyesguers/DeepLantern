# DL-020 — Lighting Fixture Kit
**Priority:** P2 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Set
Three related fixtures: adjustable work lamp, wall-mounted floodlight and compact navigation beacon. Meshes should communicate function even with Roblox lights disabled; actual light behavior is handled in Studio separately.

## Design
Thick housing, protective lens guard, heat vents, mounting bracket, cable entry and service access. Warm white for work lamps, cyan for navigation indicator, amber for caution beacon. Emissive material is restrained and does not substitute for geometry.

## Performance
500–2k triangles per fixture. Keep lenses and guards robust; pivots at mounting interface; export each fixture separately. No scripts or actual light objects included in the model deliverable.
