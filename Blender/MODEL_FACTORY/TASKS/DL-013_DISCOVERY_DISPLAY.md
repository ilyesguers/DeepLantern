# DL-013 — Discovery Specimen Display
**Priority:** P2 · **Status:** QUEUED

## Goal
A station display pedestal that presents a recovered specimen or mineral for debrief/research. It is a visual prop only; do not invent interactive behavior.

## Construction
Low pedestal with a stable base, protective clear dome or reinforced window, replaceable specimen mount, thin cyan inspection ring, warm neutral casing, dark teal support and a discreet label plate left blank for in-game UI.

## Performance
2k–5k triangles. Keep the specimen as a separate replaceable object. Transparent dome must have enough thickness to read at gameplay distance; avoid multiple overlapping transparent shells. Pivot at floor center.

## Prompt
“Stylized premium underwater research specimen display pedestal: compact stable base, reinforced clear dome or protected window, replaceable specimen mount, thin cyan inspection ring, warm off-white casing, dark teal support and small blank label plate. Designed to make an unusual mineral or biological sample feel scientifically important, clean industrial construction, optimized game mesh, UV-ready PBR, no baked text or logos.”
