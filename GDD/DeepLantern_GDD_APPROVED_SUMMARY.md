# Deep Lantern — Approved GDD Index

The full approved GDD Parts 1–10 is the source of truth. Do not replace it with this summary; use this page as an index and quick orientation.

## Game identity
Underwater exploration-adventure with functional station building. The player is Chief Expedition Officer and submarine pilot. Tone starts with wonder and curiosity and develops atmospheric, eerie mystery without becoming horror.

## Core loop
PLAN → DIVE → DECIDE → DEBRIEF → UPGRADE → GO DEEPER.

## First 10 minutes (approved concept)
Observatory Deck → starter submarine Lantern-1 → Zone 1: The Shallows → scan Glow-Guppy → collect Light Crystal → first Workshop wing → High-Lumen Spotlight Tier 1.

## Progression
Station tiers: Outpost → Station → Complex → Citadel → Deep Empire (last two are post-MVP). MVP zones: Station, Shallows, Kelp Forest, Twilight Reef. Expeditions are short, roughly 3–5 minutes, with discoveries processed at the station.

## Core rules
Exactly two currencies (Credits and Research Data), Knowledge over RNG, no idle/passive tycoon income, no gacha, no pay-to-win depth progression, no rebirths/resets/full wipes, fair rescue-based failure, solo parity and co-op target 2–4, mobile/low-end PC first-class.

## Codex
Collection stages: Sighted → Scanned → Studied → Mastered.

## Scope discipline
The approved full GDD Parts 1–10 take precedence over this index. Do not infer new mechanics from art boards.
